# Privacy Policy: Orbit Courier


**Effective date:** 15th October 2026
**Developer:** LKHour
**Contact:** l.kimhour.609@gmail.com

## Summary
Orbit Courier does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The game works fully offline.

## What stays on your device
- Your best score, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound and motion settings.
- A small local log of recent play sessions (session seed, duration, outcome, score). It is used only for your
  own device and is never sent anywhere by the game.

This data is stored in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What we do not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party services.
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
- No network communication by the game.

## Children
The game is a general-audience casual game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Changes
If a future version adds ads, purchases or analytics, this policy and the Play Data safety form will be updated
before that version is released.
